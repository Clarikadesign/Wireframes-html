§ 03 · FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Questions, answered.

Can I change plans anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle. No penalties.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data stays with you. You can export everything anytime. If you cancel, you keep your Free plan access (limited features) or you can delete your account entirely.
Can I download my content?
Absolutely. You can export your full biography, timeline, photos, and all recorded materials as PDF or video files.
Do I need tech skills to use 4EverRemember?
No. The platform is designed to be simple and intuitive. Just answer questions, and AI handles the rest. If you need help, our support team is here.
How does post-mortem access work?
With Lifetime plans, you can designate heirs who can access your legacy after you're gone. You control exactly what they see and when.
Is my data really secure and private?
Yes. We use AES-256 encryption, SOC 2 Type II certification, and GDPR compliance. Your data is audited annually. We never sell or share your information.
Is there a long-term contract?
No contracts. Monthly plans are month-to-month. Lifetime is one payment, forever. Cancel anytime with no penalties.
Do you offer customer support?
Free and Premium plans have email support. Platinum and Lifetime plans get priority support and phone access.
What happens to my legacy after I'm gone?
Your designated heirs get access according to your wishes. The legacy stays on our servers for 100 years (Lifetime plans). Family can continue adding memories and tributes.
Can I share my legacy publicly?
Yes. You control sharing settings. You can make your legacy public, private, or share with specific people. You decide.
What if I don't like it? Can I get a refund?
Monthly plans: cancel anytime, no refund but no ongoing charges. Lifetime plan: 30-day money-back guarantee. No questions asked.

Our Company

Our mission, to preserve your story, forever

4EverRemember is the #1 living digital legacy platform (powered by AI).

§ 01 · Mission
Why we exist

A modern, deeply human way to preserve who we are.

The world lacks a meaningful, modern, deeply human way to preserve the stories, personalities, and memories of the people we love.

Traditional memorial sites are static and unable to capture the emotional depth of life. More importantly, current digital legacy tools are obituary-focused.

We are an American Company based in Delaware. Our purpose is to celebrate life and give you the tools to preserve your story in your own words. For you, for your loved ones, for generations to come.

Our aim
  • Capture personality, voice, humor, and values
  • Help the living shape how they want to be remembered
  • Store future messages or legacy content
  • Remain permanent, accessible, and secure
  • Evolve over time with contributions from loved ones
